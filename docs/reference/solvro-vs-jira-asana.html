What you give up, and what you get

The honest trade. These are read from the Solvro code on the develop branch, not from a roadmap.

Solvro does not have this today

  • Notifications or an inbox. Nothing pings you when a task or deal changes. You open the page.
  • Automation rules. No "when status becomes Done, then…". Weekly Sync drafting is the only rule-based step.
  • Custom fields. The task, risk, and opportunity shapes are fixed.
  • File attachments. The customer page shows a placeholder for documents.
  • Time tracking. Work Logs is a placeholder for a future iTop integration.
  • Integrations and AI. Google sign-in and Excel import/export are the only doors in and out.
  • Native mobile app. It is an installable PWA that works down to 375 px.

Only Solvro has this on the screen

  • Customers as the spine. Every project, note, service, and deal hangs off a customer with an owning TAM, an account manager, and an SA of record.
  • Daily Review. One page per week: dated notes per project and per customer, overdue tasks, open risks, and a "Mark reviewed" stamp.
  • Weekly Sync tables. Mid-week highlights and Reflection achievements, Green / Amber / Red per customer, "Copy as table" for the Monday document.
  • Won deal to delivery project in one click and one database transaction, linked both ways.
  • Baseline lock on the Gantt with planned-versus-actual slip in days, and Indonesian public holidays shaded.
  • Internal notes never leave. Every note is INTERNAL unless marked customer-facing, and exports and search respect it.
  • Hidden means 404. A project outside your visibility does not exist to you, not even as a "no access" page.
